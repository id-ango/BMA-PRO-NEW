BMA PRO
BMA PRO is a modular ERP and accounting platform built with Blazor Server and .NET 10. It is designed to centralize business operations across sales, purchasing, inventory, receivables, payables, banking, and financial reporting in one integrated system.
The platform combines operational workflows with accounting control, helping businesses manage transactions from the first order to final financial statements. It is suitable for companies that need a structured back-office system with role-based access, reporting, and printable business documents.
Overview
Modern businesses need more than isolated tools for accounting, stock control, and transaction processing. BMA PRO provides a connected platform where operational data and financial data move together, reducing duplication, improving visibility, and supporting faster decision-making.
The application is organized into business modules, allowing teams to handle daily operations while maintaining accounting consistency across the organization.

Architecture Approach
The solution is organized into separate modules for different business domains such as:
•	Asset
•	CashBank
•	Company
•	Financial
•	Hutang (Accounts Payable)
•	Ledger
•	Order
•	Pembelian (Purchasing)
•	Penjualan (Sales)
•	Persediaan (Inventory)
•	Piutang (Accounts Receivable)
•	Reporting and supporting services
This modular approach makes the application easier to extend, maintain, and evolve as business requirements grow.

The goal of BMA PRO is to provide a practical and integrated business platform that connects operational execution with financial accountability. Instead of treating accounting, stock, and transaction management as separate activities, the system brings them together in a single workflow-oriented environment.
